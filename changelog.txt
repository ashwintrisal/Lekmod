LEKMOD PROMOTION CHANGELOG
==========================


ALTITUDE TRAINING (PROMOTION_ALTITUDE_TRAINING)
------------------------------------------------
10% movement discount in hills (hills cost 1.8 movement instead of 2.0).
- Changed: was double movement in hills (50% discount), now 10% discount via new DLL field HillsMovementDiscountPercent
- Changed: removed +10% attack and defense in hills


FAST SWIMMER (PROMOTION_PHIL_MARINE)
-------------------------------------
+2 movement while embarked.
- Changed: embarked movement bonus 3 -> 2


SKYRANGER / EXTENDED PARADROP (PROMOTION_EXTENDED_PARADROP)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Allows paradrops with a range of 100 hexes.
Eligible: paratroopers (UNITCOMBAT_PARADROPPER).
- Changed: paradrop range 40 -> 100


HEAL INSTANTLY (PROMOTION_INSTA_HEAL)
--------------------------------------
Heals the unit for 40 HP instantly when activated. One-time use per acquisition.
Eligible: all unit types (unchanged).
- Changed: heal amount 50 -> 40


COASTAL RAIDER LINE (PROMOTION_COASTAL_RAIDER_1/2/3)
------------------------------------------------------
+25% combat strength when attacking cities per tier.
Eligible: naval melee (UNITCOMBAT_NAVALMELEE).
- Changed: city attack bonus per tier was 15/15/20, now 25/25/25
- Changed: removed gold looting on city attack (CityAttackPlunderModifier was 33/33/34, now 0)


BOARDING PARTY LINE (PROMOTION_BOARDING_PARTY_1/2/3)
------------------------------------------------------
+25% combat strength when attacking naval units (DOMAIN_SEA) per tier.
Eligible: naval melee (UNITCOMBAT_NAVALMELEE).
- Changed: bonus per tier 15% -> 25%


ARMOR PLATING LINE (PROMOTION_ARMOR_PLATING_1/2/3)
----------------------------------------------------
3-tier line, no prerequisites for tier 1.
Eligible: carriers (UNITCOMBAT_CARRIER), naval melee (UNITCOMBAT_NAVALMELEE), helicopters (UNITCOMBAT_HELICOPTER).
- Changed: was carrier-only; now also available to naval melee and helicopters.
Unlocks (shared with other naval melee lines):
- Armor Plating 1 -> Sentry, Mobility (unchanged, already wired)
- Armor Plating 2 -> Supply (unchanged, already wired)
- Armor Plating 3 -> Logistics (added; parity with Coastal Raider 3 / Boarding Party 3)
Unlocks (helicopter-specific, added):
- Armor Plating 2 -> Heli Repair, Air Logistics (parity with Heli Ambush 2 / Heli Mobility 2 / Anti-Naval Heli 2)


DISCIPLINE (PROMOTION_DISCIPLINE / PROMOTION_DISCIPLINE_AIR)
---------------------------------------------------------------
NEW. Granted by policy (not player-selectable). Two internal variants, same icon.
- PROMOTION_DISCIPLINE: +50% XP gained. Applies to all non-air units (melee, ranged, gunpowder, mounted, armor, naval, helicopter, etc.).
- PROMOTION_DISCIPLINE_AIR: +25% XP gained. Applies to air units (fighters and bombers, UNITCOMBAT_AIR) only.
Policy wiring (Honor finisher / Iron Fist) pending.


SWIFT TRANSPORT (PROMOTION_SWIFT_TRANSPORT)
-------------------------------------------
NEW. Embarked units sharing this ship's tile at the start of a turn gain this ship's movement for that turn (movement only upgraded, never reduced).
- Added: EmbarkedUnitReceivesMovement=true
- Note: unit eligibility and assignment wired separately; not player-pickable


MOBILITY (PROMOTION_MOBILITY)
------------------------------
Now accessible to melee, gunpowder, and paratroopers via earlier prerequisites.
- Added UnitCombatTypes: MELEE, GUN, PARADROPPER
- Added prerequisites: Drill 2, Shock 2, Ambush Formation 2, Amphibious 2
- Note: Amphibious 2 prereq is inert until that line gets unit type eligibility defined
- Existing prereqs (Drill 3, Shock 3, etc.) unchanged


FEAR AURA (PROMOTION_FEAR_AURA)
--------------------------------
NEW. Enemies within 1 tile suffer -15% combat strength.
- Added: NearbyEnemyCombatMod=-15, NearbyEnemyCombatRange=1
- Note: no domain restriction (affects all unit types in range); Feared Elephant equivalent is -10%


ZEALOT (PROMOTION_ZEALOT)
-------------------------
NEW. Killing a unit yields Faith equal to their Combat Strength (max 30).
- Added: UnitPromotions_YieldFromKills entry (YIELD_FAITH, Yield=100%, Max=30)
- Added: free promotion for UNIT_CELT_PICTISH_WARRIOR and UNIT_ISRAEL_MACCABEE
- Removed: unit-level Unit_YieldFromKills YIELD_FAITH entries for both units (replaced by this promotion)
- Note: previous Pictish Warrior behavior had no cap; now capped at 30


EMBARKED UNIT DEFENSE (FORMULA CHANGE)
---------------------------------------
Embarked unit defense is now 40% of the unit's highest combat strength (melee or ranged), scaled by EmbarkDefenseModifier. Previously era-based (fixed value per era regardless of unit strength).
- Changed: GetEmbarkedUnitDefense() formula from era lookup to max(meleCS, rangedCS) * 40%


AMPHIBIOUS LINE (PROMOTION_AMPHIBIOUS_1/2/3)
---------------------------------------------
NEW 3-tier line replacing vanilla PROMOTION_AMPHIBIOUS. No prerequisites for tier 1.
Eligible: melee (UNITCOMBAT_MELEE), gunpowder (UNITCOMBAT_GUN), mounted (UNITCOMBAT_MOUNTED), armor (UNITCOMBAT_ARMOR), ranged (UNITCOMBAT_ARCHER), mounted ranged (UNITCOMBAT_MOUNTED_RANGED).
Tier 1 unlocks Tier 2; Tier 2 unlocks Tier 3 (and also unlocks Mobility).
- Amphibious I: No river attack penalty. Embarked CS = 70% of base (EmbarkDefenseModifier=75 on new formula). Embarking costs 1 MP (ZOC applies); at most 1 MP remaining after.
- Amphibious II: +1 embarked movement. Disembarking costs 1 MP (ZOC applies); at most 1 MP remaining after.
- Amphibious III: No penalty attacking from water (Amphib). +1 embarked movement. At most 2 MP remaining after domain change. Can heal while embarked under normal land healing rules.
- Removed: vanilla PROMOTION_AMPHIBIOUS (CannotBeChosen=true, no effects) — units that held it retain it as a dummy.
- New DLL fields: EmbarkFlatCost, DisembarkFlatCost, MaxMovesAfterDomainChange, HealWhileEmbarked (promotion-level).


WOODSMAN (PROMOTION_WOODSMAN)
------------------------------
Double movement in forest and jungle. +15% attack and defense in forest and jungle.
Eligible: melee (UNITCOMBAT_MELEE), gunpowder (UNITCOMBAT_GUN), paratroopers (UNITCOMBAT_PARADROPPER).
Requires: Drill 1.
- Changed: prerequisite was Shock 2 OR Drill 2, now Drill 1 only
