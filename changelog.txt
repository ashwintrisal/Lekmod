==========================

JESUIT EDUCATION (BELIEF_JESUIT_EDUCATION) & WORK ETHIC (BELIEF_WORK_ETHIQ)
==========================================================================
Both are Reformation beliefs.

SHARED CHANGES (both beliefs):
- Added: BlockAutoProphetSpawn=true — AI no longer auto-saves faith for prophets when this reformation is active (same mechanic as Industrial era)
- Changed: FaithCost normalized to 1.3× ProductionCost (rounded to nearest 5) for all affected buildings and their unique variants

JESUIT EDUCATION:
Buildings affected: Library, University, Observatory, Public School, Research Lab (and all civ-unique variants)
Faith cost changes: Library 60→100, University 220→210, Observatory 260 (unchanged), Public School 380→390, Research Lab 630→650
Unique variants: Paper Maker 60→100, Royal Library 60→100, Akkad Library 48→80, Wat 165→155, Ayyubids University 220→210, Halkevleri 240→310, Armenia Observatory (unchanged 260)
- Added: +2 Faith yield from each of the 5 science building classes (Library, University, Observatory, Public School, Laboratory)
- New DLL: LEKMOD_BLOCK_PROPHET_SPAWN_BELIEF — BlockAutoProphetSpawn column in Beliefs table

WORK ETHIC:
Buildings affected: Workshop, Windmill, Factory, Hydro Plant (and all civ-unique variants)
Faith cost changes: Workshop 200→155, Windmill 320→325, Factory 450→470, Hydro Plant 533→470
Unique variants: Longhouse (unchanged 100), Coralshop 200→155, Prussian Factory 340→350, Cooperative 450→470, Coffee House 188→195, Hacienda 320→325
- Added: Workers can now be purchased with Faith (cost = 1.3× production = ~65 faith base, scaled by game speed)
- New DLL: LEKMOD_FAITH_PURCHASE_UNIT_CLASS_BELIEF — Belief_FaithPurchaseUnitClass table; CvBeliefEntry/CvReligionBeliefs::IsFaithPurchaseUnitClassEnabled; dynamic cost computed in CvCity::GetFaithPurchaseCost(UnitTypes) as ProductionCost×130/100 when unit class is enabled by belief


UNDERGROUND SECT (BELIEF_UNDERGROUND_SECT)
------------------------------------------
Reformation belief. Three new mechanics:

SPY FROM RENAISSANCE (LEKMOD_SPY_FROM_RENAISSANCE_BELIEF):

NON-DOMINANT FOLLOWER YIELD (LEKMOD_NON_DOMINANT_FOLLOWER_YIELD_BELIEF):
- Added: +2 gold per turn per city where this religion has followers but is NOT the majority religion
- Added: +2 science per turn per city where this religion has followers but is NOT the majority religion

HOUSES OF WORSHIP (BELIEF_HOUSES_OF_WORSHIP)

City-level culture bonus scales with the count of Houses present: 1st = +4 Culture, 2nd adds +2 more (total 6), 3rd adds nothing.
New DLL: LEKMOD_HOUSES_OF_WORSHIP_CULTURE — computed in CvCity::processBuilding; tracks presence changes for each of the 3 classes.

ART HALL (BUILDING_ARTIST_HOUSE):

- Removed: old +4 Faith, +1 Culture yields

WRITING AUDITORIUM (BUILDING_WRITER_HOUSE):

VOCAL CHAMBER (BUILDING_MUSIC_HOUSE):

ANCIENT RUINS (GOODY HUT) OVERHAUL
====================================
All ruin rewards are now era-scaled. New DLL columns added to GoodyHuts table.

FOOD (GOODY_FOOD)
- Changed: fixed food (10-10 + 2/pop) replaced by era-scaled formula: 7 + floor(2 * pop * era * 1.1), applied to capital
- Added: unit receives PROMOTION_GLUTTON (-10% combat strength, lost on upgrade)

UPGRADE (GOODY_UPGRADE_UNIT)
- Changed: now available from turn 6 (was turn 0)
- Added: unit loses 15 HP on upgrade (cannot kill the unit)

SCIENCE (GOODY_TECH)
- Changed: no longer grants a free technology
- Added: yields X science (X = rand(20..30) * era, capped at 90) toward current research (overflow if none)
- Added: unit loses X HP (minimum 1 HP remaining) and gains X/2 XP

MOBILITY (GOODY_MOBILITY, formerly GOODY_EXPERIENCE_MOVE)
- Changed: renamed from GOODY_EXPERIENCE_MOVE
- Changed: no longer grants 15 XP or PROMOTION_EXTRA_MOVES_RUIN
- Added: grants PROMOTION_MOBILITY instead
- Added: ancient era only (disabled from Classical era onward)

MAP (GOODY_MAP)
- Changed: reveal radius 5 -> 6, per-tile reveal probability 100% -> 75% (jagged reveal)
- Added: +6 XP to the unit that popped the ruin

CULTURE (GOODY_CULTURE)
- Changed: flat 20 culture replaced by era-scaled formula: turn * era
- Changed: available from turn 6 (was turn 0; previously delayed to turn 12 via DLL)

PANTHEON FAITH (GOODY_PANTHEON_FAITH)
- Changed: flat faith 5 -> 14
- Added: only available if player has 0 faith stockpile
- Added: available from turn 6 (was turn 0)
- Changed: re-enabled at all difficulties (was commented out in HandicapInfo_Goodies)

RANDOM FAITH (GOODY_RANDOM_FAITH)
- Changed: flat faith (30-34) replaced by era-scaled formula: turn * 2 * era
- Added: heals unit to full HP

GOLD (GOODY_GOLD)
- Changed: fixed gold (50 + rand rolls) replaced by era-scaled formula: 45 + rand(1..10) * 5 * era
- Added: if total gold >= 80, spawns one era-appropriate barbarian on an adjacent tile
  (ancient=Warrior, classical=Swordsman, medieval=Knight, renaissance=Lancer, industrial=Gatling Gun)

NEW PROMOTION: GLUTTON (PROMOTION_GLUTTON)
- Internal only (CannotBeChosen), lost on upgrade
- -10% combat strength

EMBARKED UNIT DEFENSE (FORMULA CHANGE)
---------------------------------------
Embarked unit defense is now 40% of the unit's highest combat strength (melee or ranged), scaled by EmbarkDefenseModifier. Previously era-based (fixed value per era regardless of unit strength).
- Changed: GetEmbarkedUnitDefense() formula from era lookup to max(meleCS, rangedCS) * 40%


AMPHIBIOUS LINE (PROMOTION_AMPHIBIOUS_1/2/3)
---------------------------------------------
NEW 3-tier line replacing vanilla PROMOTION_AMPHIBIOUS. No prerequisites for tier 1.
Eligible: melee (UNITCOMBAT_MELEE), gunpowder (UNITCOMBAT_GUN), mounted (UNITCOMBAT_MOUNTED), armor (UNITCOMBAT_ARMOR), ranged (UNITCOMBAT_ARCHER), mounted ranged (UNITCOMBAT_MOUNTED_RANGED).
Tier 1 unlocks Tier 2; Tier 2 unlocks Tier 3 (and also unlocks Mobility).
- Amphibious I: No river attack penalty. Embarked CS = 70% of base (EmbarkDefenseModifier=75 on new formula). Embarking costs 1 MP (ZOC applies); at most 1 MP remaining after.
- Amphibious II: +1 embarked movement. Disembarking costs 1 MP (ZOC applies); at most 1 MP remaining after.
- Amphibious III: No penalty attacking from water (Amphib). +1 embarked movement. At most 2 MP remaining after domain change. Can heal while embarked under normal land healing rules.
- Removed: vanilla PROMOTION_AMPHIBIOUS (CannotBeChosen=true, no effects) — units that held it retain it as a dummy.
- New DLL fields: EmbarkFlatCost, DisembarkFlatCost, MaxMovesAfterDomainChange, HealWhileEmbarked (promotion-level).


MOUNTAINEERING (PROMOTION_MOUNTAINEERING)
------------------------------------------
NEW. Allows mountain crossing. Units ending their turn on a mountain take 50 damage (existing mechanic, same as Carthage).
Eligible: melee (UNITCOMBAT_MELEE), gunpowder (UNITCOMBAT_GUN).
Requires (any): Drill 2, Altitude Training.
- Added: CanCrossMountains=true (new DLL field; patches canEnterTerrain mountain gate)
- The 50 mountain damage reuses the existing end-of-turn attrition check (line 6473 CvUnit.cpp) — no new code needed

Eligible: siege (UNITCOMBAT_SIEGE), bomber (UNITCOMBAT_BOMBER).

- Added: CityAttack=33 (existing field), CitySplashDamage=10 (new DLL field)
- New DLL field CitySplashDamage triggers in ResolveRangedUnitVsCombat and ResolveAirUnitVsCombat after city takes damage

SAMBUCA (PROMOTION_SAMBUCA)
---------------------------------
NEW. Naval melee variant of the Assyrian Siege Tower's Sapper promotion.
Eligible: naval melee (UNITCOMBAT_NAVALMELEE).
Requires: Coastal Raider I (PROMOTION_COASTAL_RAIDER_1).
Effects: Sapper=true (reduces enemy city defense), -33% Combat Strength on defense, -1 Movement.
- Note: PROMOTION_SAPPER (Siege Tower) is unchanged; this is a separate choosable promotion
  with a different display name but with balance penalties for the naval melee context.
- Penalties are built into PROMOTION_SAMBUCA; no separate PROMOTION_PENALTY_ON_DEFENSE
  was created (vanilla Civ5 has no promotion-level FreePromotion field to auto-grant one).


CARPET BOMBING (PROMOTION_CARPET_BOMBING)
------------------------------------------
NEW. Each attack deals 20% of inflicted damage to all non-air units on adjacent tiles (including friendlies).
Eligible: bombers (UNITCOMBAT_BOMBER).
Requires: Bombardment 2.
- Added: CarpetBombing=true (new DLL field)
- War declaration: hitting a City State unit triggers declareWar(); hitting a major civ unit not at war deals damage without declaration
- Interception: iAttackerDamageInflicted is already interception-adjusted before splash is computed, so reduced bombing naturally reduces splash
- Splash fires in ResolveAirUnitVsCombat for both city-target and unit-target branches


SUPPLY SHIP (PROMOTION_SUPPLY_SHIP)
------------------------------------
NEW. Adjacent naval units may heal outside friendly territory (normally impossible without HealOutsideFriendly).
Adjacent non-air units heal +10 HP per turn when outside friendly territory.
Eligible: naval melee (UNITCOMBAT_NAVALMELEE), naval ranged (UNITCOMBAT_NAVALRANGED).
Requires: Coastal Raider 2, Boarding Party 2, or Armor Plating 2 (any one).
- Added: AdjacentTileHealOutsideFriendly=10 (new DLL field)
- healRate() DOMAIN_SEA early-return is bypassed when an adjacent allied unit has AdjacentTileHealOutsideFriendly > 0
- canHeal() DOMAIN_SEA gate is also bypassed in the same way
- The +10 bonus from AdjacentTileHealOutsideFriendly is only applied when the healing unit is outside friendly territory and not air domain
