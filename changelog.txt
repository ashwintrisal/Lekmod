LEKMOD PROMOTION CHANGELOG
==========================


MISCELLANEOUS
=============

CIRCUMNAVIGATION AWARDS 200 GOLD WITH GLOBAL NOTIFICATION
----------------------------------------------------------
- First team to circumnavigate the globe earns 200 gold per player on the team
- All players receive a notification (met: named civ; unmet: unknown civ)
- DLL hook in CvTeam.cpp: testCircumnavigated() after existing player loop
- New text key TXT_KEY_MISC_YOU_CIRC_GLOBE_GOLD added to CIV5Units_Mongol.xml

WLTKD DURATION AND DEMAND INTERVAL FIXED TO 10 TURNS
------------------------------------------------------
- We Love The King Day duration hardcoded to 10 turns (was ~13 on quick after speed scaling)
- Demanded luxury switches every 10 turns (was ~26 on quick after speed scaling)
- UAE wonder UA kingsday bonus also set to 10 turns (was 15)
- DLL hooks in CvCity.cpp: DoTestResourceDemanded() and DoPickResourceDemanded()
- Lua change in Lekmod_uae.lua: kings_day_length = 10

GIANT DEATH ROBOT CAN PILLAGE (HEALS 0 HP)
--------------------------------------------
- UNIT_MECH can now pillage but receives no HP healing from it
- New XML bool NoHealFromPillage added to Units table; new getter CvUnitEntry::IsNoHealFromPillage()
- DLL hook in CvUnit.cpp: pillage() skips heal when IsNoHealFromPillage() is true

GREAT GENERALS ALWAYS SPAWN IN CAPITAL
----------------------------------------
- Great Generals now always spawn in the capital city
- Previously spawned in a semi-random highest-ranked city (yield + population + random noise)
- DLL hook in CvPlayer.cpp: replaced city-selection loop with getCapitalCity()


MISCELLANEOUS
=============

NAVAL UNITS NO LONGER EXERT ZOC AGAINST LAND UNITS
-----------------------------------------------------
- Naval units no longer apply zone of control to land units
- Previously DOMAIN_SEA units could slow DOMAIN_LAND units due to an explicit cross-domain exception
- DLL hook in CvUnitMovement.cpp: LEKMOD_NO_NAVAL_ZOC_VS_LAND

FORTIFIED RANGED LAND UNITS BENEFIT FROM FORTIFICATION BONUS AGAINST RANGED ATTACKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Ranged land units now apply their fortification bonus when defending against ranged attacks
- Previously their defender strength was calculated from ranged combat strength (no fortification)
- DLL hook in CvUnit.cpp: LEKMOD_RANGED_FORTIFY_BONUS


MISCELLANEOUS
=============

TRIBUTE RANGE FIXED TO 5
--------------------------
- City-state bully/tribute comparison radius is now hardcoded to 5 tiles regardless of map size
- Previously scaled as max(gridWidth / 10, 5), which grew on larger maps
- DLL hook in CvMinorCivAI.cpp: LEKMOD_TRIBUTE_RANGE_FIXED


OPEN BORDERS DURATION 10 TURNS
--------------------------------
- Open borders agreements now last exactly 10 turns regardless of game speed
- Tooltip updated to show "10 turns" instead of the game-speed-scaled value
- DLL hook in CvDealClasses.cpp: LEKMOD_OPEN_BORDERS_FIXED_DURATION


MISCELLANEOUS
=============

PROPHET KILLED ON CAPTURE
--------------------------
- Great Prophets are now killed when captured instead of changing ownership (same as other great people)
- Removed <Capture>UNITCLASS_PROPHET</Capture> from UNIT_PROPHET XML row

AMPHIBIOUS LINE (PROMOTION_AMPHIBIOUS_1/2/3)
---------------------------------------------
NEW 3-tier line replacing vanilla PROMOTION_AMPHIBIOUS. No prerequisites for tier 1.
Eligible: melee (UNITCOMBAT_MELEE), gunpowder (UNITCOMBAT_GUN), mounted (UNITCOMBAT_MOUNTED), armor (UNITCOMBAT_ARMOR), ranged (UNITCOMBAT_ARCHER), mounted ranged (UNITCOMBAT_MOUNTED_RANGED).
Tier 1 unlocks Tier 2; Tier 2 unlocks Tier 3 (and also unlocks Mobility).
- Amphibious I: No river attack penalty. Embarked CS = 70% of base (EmbarkDefenseModifier=75 on new formula). Embarking costs 1 MP (ZOC applies); at most 1 MP remaining after.
- Amphibious II: +1 embarked movement. Disembarking costs 1 MP (ZOC applies); at most 1 MP remaining after.
- Amphibious III: No penalty attacking from water (Amphib). +1 embarked movement. At most 2 MP remaining after domain change. Can heal while embarked under normal land healing rules.
- Removed: vanilla PROMOTION_AMPHIBIOUS (CannotBeChosen=true, no effects) — units that held it retain it as a dummy.
- New DLL fields: EmbarkFlatCost, DisembarkFlatCost, MaxMovesAfterDomainChange, HealWhileEmbarked (promotion-level).
