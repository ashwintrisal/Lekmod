CITY-STATE PERSONAL QUEST RECYCLING (DLL)
------------------------------------------
- Personal quests that remain incomplete for 15 turns (quick speed) are revoked and immediately
  replaced with a new randomly-weighted personal quest from the valid pool
- Recycling is orthogonal to the quest countdown; the replacement takes the existing slot
- Controlled by `MINOR_CIV_PERSONAL_QUEST_RECYCLE_TURNS` (XML: 23, = 15 at quick speed)
- Implemented in `DoRecyclePersonalQuestsForPlayer()` called per-player from `DoTurnQuests()`

CITY-STATE WAR DECLARATION PENALTIES
-------------------------------------
- Declaring war on city-states now tracks four escalating tiers per player:
  antagonist (1), provocateur (2), aggressor (3), warmonger (4+)
- Wary: each CS you declared war on suffers a resting influence penalty
  (antagonist: -10; provocateur/aggressor/warmonger: -30)
- Alienated: each CS you declared war on has an increased influence decay rate
  (antagonist: +50%; provocateur/aggressor/warmonger: +100%)
- Provocateur: the 2 closest met CSs you have not declared war on become wary (-20)
- Aggressor: all CSs become wary (-20) and alienated (+50%)
- Warmonger: all CSs become wary (-30) and alienated (+100%)
- Ally exception: CSs that are currently your ally do not receive additional penalties
- Declaring war 3 times on the same CS makes it nemesis — that CS will never accept peace

AMPHIBIOUS LINE (PROMOTION_AMPHIBIOUS_1/2/3)
---------------------------------------------
NEW 3-tier line replacing vanilla PROMOTION_AMPHIBIOUS. No prerequisites for tier 1.
Eligible: melee (UNITCOMBAT_MELEE), gunpowder (UNITCOMBAT_GUN), mounted (UNITCOMBAT_MOUNTED), armor (UNITCOMBAT_ARMOR), ranged (UNITCOMBAT_ARCHER), mounted ranged (UNITCOMBAT_MOUNTED_RANGED).
Tier 1 unlocks Tier 2; Tier 2 unlocks Tier 3 (and also unlocks Mobility).
- Amphibious I: No river attack penalty. Embarked CS = 70% of base (EmbarkDefenseModifier=75 on new formula). Embarking costs 1 MP (ZOC applies); at most 1 MP remaining after.
- Amphibious II: +1 embarked movement. Disembarking costs 1 MP (ZOC applies); at most 1 MP remaining after.
- Amphibious III: No penalty attacking from water (Amphib). +1 embarked movement. At most 2 MP remaining after domain change. Can heal while embarked under normal land healing rules.
- Removed: vanilla PROMOTION_AMPHIBIOUS (CannotBeChosen=true, no effects) — units that held it retain it as a dummy.
- New DLL fields: EmbarkFlatCost, DisembarkFlatCost, MaxMovesAfterDomainChange, HealWhileEmbarked (promotion-level).
