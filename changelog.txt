LEKMOD PROMOTION CHANGELOG
==========================


SKYRANGER / EXTENDED PARADROP (PROMOTION_EXTENDED_PARADROP)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Allows paradrops with a range of 100 hexes.
Eligible: paratroopers (UNITCOMBAT_PARADROPPER).
- Changed: paradrop range 40 -> 100


HEAL INSTANTLY (PROMOTION_INSTA_HEAL)
--------------------------------------
Heals the unit for 40 HP instantly when activated. One-time use per acquisition.
Eligible: all unit types (unchanged).
- Changed: heal amount 50 -> 40


COASTAL RAIDER LINE (PROMOTION_COASTAL_RAIDER_1/2/3)
------------------------------------------------------
+25% combat strength when attacking cities per tier.
Eligible: naval melee (UNITCOMBAT_NAVALMELEE).
- Changed: city attack bonus per tier was 15/15/20, now 25/25/25
- Changed: removed gold looting on city attack (CityAttackPlunderModifier was 33/33/34, now 0)


BOARDING PARTY LINE (PROMOTION_BOARDING_PARTY_1/2/3)
------------------------------------------------------
+25% combat strength when attacking naval units (DOMAIN_SEA) per tier.
Eligible: naval melee (UNITCOMBAT_NAVALMELEE).
- Changed: bonus per tier 15% -> 25%


ARMOR PLATING LINE (PROMOTION_ARMOR_PLATING_1/2/3)
----------------------------------------------------
3-tier line, no prerequisites for tier 1.
Eligible: carriers (UNITCOMBAT_CARRIER), naval melee (UNITCOMBAT_NAVALMELEE), helicopters (UNITCOMBAT_HELICOPTER).
- Changed: was carrier-only; now also available to naval melee and helicopters.
Unlocks (shared with other naval melee lines):
- Armor Plating 1 -> Sentry, Mobility (unchanged, already wired)
- Armor Plating 2 -> Supply (unchanged, already wired)
- Armor Plating 3 -> Logistics (added; parity with Coastal Raider 3 / Boarding Party 3)
Unlocks (helicopter-specific, added):
- Armor Plating 2 -> Heli Repair, Air Logistics (parity with Heli Ambush 2 / Heli Mobility 2 / Anti-Naval Heli 2)


DISCIPLINE (PROMOTION_DISCIPLINE / PROMOTION_DISCIPLINE_AIR)
---------------------------------------------------------------
NEW. Granted by policy (not player-selectable). Two internal variants, same icon.
- PROMOTION_DISCIPLINE: +50% XP gained. Applies to all non-air units (melee, ranged, gunpowder, mounted, armor, naval, helicopter, etc.).
- PROMOTION_DISCIPLINE_AIR: +25% XP gained. Applies to air units (fighters and bombers, UNITCOMBAT_AIR) only.
Policy wiring (Honor finisher / Iron Fist) pending.


WOODSMAN (PROMOTION_WOODSMAN)
------------------------------
Double movement in forest and jungle. +15% attack and defense in forest and jungle.
Eligible: melee (UNITCOMBAT_MELEE), gunpowder (UNITCOMBAT_GUN), paratroopers (UNITCOMBAT_PARADROPPER).
Requires: Drill 1.
- Changed: prerequisite was Shock 2 OR Drill 2, now Drill 1 only
