LEKMOD PROMOTION CHANGELOG
==========================


HEAL INSTANTLY (PROMOTION_INSTA_HEAL)
--------------------------------------
Heals the unit for 40 HP instantly when activated. One-time use per acquisition.
Eligible: all unit types (unchanged).
- Changed: heal amount 50 -> 40


WOODSMAN (PROMOTION_WOODSMAN)
------------------------------
Double movement in forest and jungle. +15% attack and defense in forest and jungle.
Eligible: melee (UNITCOMBAT_MELEE), gunpowder (UNITCOMBAT_GUN), paratroopers (UNITCOMBAT_PARADROPPER).
Requires: Drill 1.
- Changed: prerequisite was Shock 2 OR Drill 2, now Drill 1 only
